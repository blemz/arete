# SOFE Methodology - Sistema de Objetivos Fundamentais da Educação

## Overview

**SOFE (Sistema de Objetivos Fundamentais da Educação)** is Víctor García Hoz's integrated system for organizing educational objectives. It unifies pedagogical taxonomies (Bloom - cognitive, Kratchwohl - affective, Harrow - psychomotor) into a single three-dimensional framework.

**Source:** "O significado da excelência educativa na educação personalizada - Parte 2" by Cláudio Rigo (expert in Personalized Education)

---

## Core Principle

**Educational objectives must be organized in a SYSTEM that provides unity to the educational process.** Without this system, educational efforts fragment knowledge and make it nearly impossible for students to achieve unity in their lives.

---

## Foundation: Semantic Research

García Hoz (1976, 1977, 1978) studied verbal expressions across different sciences and discovered:
- Sciences differ by their **material object** (what they study)
- Sciences coincide greatly in **functions/activities** that knowledge and scientific learning imply
- Scientific vocabulary offers a support point to go beyond the simple object of science and reach the **structure and functionality of scientific thought**

## The Six Phases of Learning and Knowledge

### Two Stages (Etapas)

#### **PERCEPTIVE STAGE (Etapa Perceptiva)** - "ad intra" movement (inward)

Knowledge moves from outside to inside, reality introduces a representation of itself into us.

1. **Receptiva (Receptive Phase) - Observação**
   - **Great Functions:** Observar, Ler, Escutar
   - **Example Specifications:** 
     - Observar: Advertir, aprender, buscar, contemplar, examinar, fixar-se, interessar-se, olhar, notar, perceber, ver, vigiar
     - Ler: Interpretar, traduzir
     - Escutar: Ouvir

2. **Reflexiva (Reflective Phase) - Análise e Relação**
   - **Great Functions:** Analisar, Contar, Relacionar, Julgar
   - **Example Specifications:**
     - Analisar: Isolar, afastar, buscar, decompor, diferenciar, distinguir, separar
     - Contar: Calcular, dividir, enumerar, medir, multiplicar, pesar, repartir, subtrair, somar
     - Relacionar: Agrupar, ajustar, antepor, articular, designar, classificar, colocar, comparar, deduzir, derivar, encabeçar, enlaçar, igualar, implicar, incluir, juntar, ordenar, organizar, explicar, reunir, situar
     - Julgar: Apreciar, considerar, criticar, duvidar, estimar, pensar, precisar, refletir

3. **Aquisitiva (Acquisitive Phase) - Síntese e Memorização**
   - **Great Functions:** Definir, Identificar, Resumir, Memorizar
   - **Example Specifications:**
     - Definir: Abarcar, atribuir, compreender, conter, escrever, determinar, entender, resolver, saber, significar
     - Identificar: Aprovar, qualificar, confirmar, constatar, descobrir, encontrar, reconhecer
     - Resumir: Acabar, afirmar, concentrar, concretar, escolher, excluir, finalizar, fundir, limitar, reduzir, renunciar, resolver, simplificar, sintetizar, unificar, unir
     - Memorizar: Aceitar, lembrar-se, admitir, adquirir, aprender, assegurar

**Chronological Order:** These three phases present a certain chronological order.

---

#### **REACTIVE STAGE (Etapa Reativa)** - "ad extra" movement (outward)

Knowledge becomes effective by going out to the external world.

4. **Comunicativa (Communicative Phase) - Expressão**
   - **Great Functions:** Explicar, Falar, Escrever, Desenhar, Atuar
   - **Example Specifications:**
     - Explicar: Aclarar, anunciar, caracterizar, convencer, descrever, enunciar, expor, formular, indicar, instruir, justificar, manifestar, mostrar, narrar, opinar, precisar, apresentar, assinalar, sugerir
     - Falar: Dizer, ditar, nomear, pregar, prometer, pronunciar, protestar, referir, reiterar, rogar (todas as atividades assinaladas em "falar" também se podem "escrever")
     - Escrever: Consignar, assinar, imprimir, publicar, redigir, resenhar, subscrever, titular, transcrever
     - Desenhar: Adornar, colorir, confeccionar, esboçar, iluminar, ilustrar, pintar, traçar, riscar
     - Atuar (3 sub-modes):
       - Movimento corporal: Andar, correr, circular, subir...
       - Modo de usar as coisas: Cuidar, guardar, ordenar, jogar...
       - Modo de trabalhar: Colaborar, tramitar, iniciar, prosseguir, terminar...

5. **Extensiva (Extensive Phase) - Ampliação e Criação**
   - **Great Functions:** Perguntar, Ampliar, Mudar, Imaginar, Criar
   - **Example Specifications:**
     - Perguntar: Apelar, consultar, dialogar, propor, referir
     - Ampliar: Abrir, alargar, acrescentar, aportar, aumentar, avançar, complicar, crescer, desenvolver, estender, incorporar, integrar, intercalar, introduzir, prolongar, terminar
     - Mudar: Alterar, anular, consertar, converter, corrigir, intercambiar, modificar, melhorar, aperfeiçoar, polir, retificar, reformar, refazer, remediar, reparar, suprir, substituir, transformar, transportar, transladar, variar
     - Imaginar: Adivinhar, figurar, projetar, representar, supor, prever
     - Criar: Iluminar, combinar, conceber, descobrir, achar, improvisar, inspirar, inventar, investigar

6. **Prática (Practical Phase) - Aplicação e Realização**
   - **Great Functions:** Valorizar, Eleger, Reproduzir, Utilizar, Construir
   - **Example Specifications:**
     - Valorizar: Admirar, apreciar, aprovar, estimar, avaliar
     - Eleger: Adquirir, decidir, escolher, excluir, preferir, rejeitar, renunciar, selecionar
     - Reproduzir: Citar, recordar, refletir, repetir, representar, traduzir
     - Utilizar: Adaptar, aplicar, aproveitar, dedicar, dispor, empregar, ensaiar, praticar, usar
     - Construir: Armar, compor, confeccionar, constituir, desfazer, destruir, dirigir, executar, elaborar, fabricar, formar, fundar, montar, operar, participar, preparar, produzir, realizar, reconstruir, situar, trabalhar

**No Chronological Order:** Unlike the perceptive stage, the reactive stage does not have a clear chronological order among its phases.

## Organic Cooperation

**Critical principle:** The six phases cooperate organically:
- The exercise of functions from one phase offers material for others
  - Example: Observation offers material for reflection
- Functions from one phase reinforce others
  - Example: Application reinforces memory

**Teacher's Challenge:** Find a way to coordinate diverse educational objectives without complicating the teacher's task further or breaking the unity of the educational process.

---

## Two Types of Educational Objectives

### I. OBJETIVOS DE APRENDIZAGEM ESPECÍFICOS (Specific Learning Objectives)

**Definition:** Those determined by all and each of the teaching subjects considered necessary in any curriculum.

**Characteristics:**
- Different knowledge areas involve or require specific approaches for content types:
  - **Conceptual** (humanities/social sciences)
  - **Procedural** (exact sciences)
- This largely defines the teacher's selection of the most appropriate didactic tools for different learning forms

**Role in SOFE:** Represent the KNOWLEDGE dimension (Conhecimentos)

---

### II. OBJETIVOS DE DESENVOLVIMENTO (Development Objectives)

**Definition:** Objectives derived from the six phases of the learning and knowledge process, responsible for the integral development of the student.

**Great Functions:** Actions related to the intellectual process that the teacher must provoke and observe if they happen in the student - otherwise, knowledge acquisition is frustrated.

**Role in SOFE:** Represent the APTITUDES/SKILLS dimension (Aptidões)

---

## The Three-Dimensional SOFE Model

García Hoz (2018, p. 178) uses a three-dimensional model that contemplates all requirements of educational activity:

### **Three Components:**

1. **CONHECIMENTOS (Knowledge)**
   - Specific learning objectives for each subject
   - Mapped to curriculum standards (BNCC in Brazil)
   - Categories:
     - C.a. - Conhecimentos artísticos
     - C.e. - Conhecimentos éticos
     - C.t. - Conhecimentos técnicos
     - C.i.f. - Ciências formais
     - C.i.r. - Ciências reais
     - Cr.v. - Critérios valorativos
     - I.g. - Informação geral

2. **APTIDÕES (Aptitudes/Skills)**
   - Development objectives based on the 6 phases
   - Mapped to cognitive functions
   - Phases:
     - Per. - Perceptiva (fase)
     - Ref. - Reflexiva (fase)
     - Cr. - Criativa (fase)
     - Ret. - Retentiva (fase)
     - E.s. - Expressão simbólica
     - E.p. - Expressão prática

3. **VALORES (Values/Virtues)**
   - Human formation objectives
   - Developed through well-done work
   - Categories:
     - Vit. - Vitais (valores)
     - Est. - Estéticos (valores)
     - Téc. - Técnicos (valores)
     - Int. - Intelectuais (valores)
     - Pr. - Prudenciais (valores)
     - Rel. - Religiosos (valores)
     - Soc. - Sociais (valores)
     - Ind. - Individuais (valores)

## Practical Planning Tool: Double-Entry Table

**Tool:** "Quadro de planejamento de atividades" (Activity Planning Table)

### Structure:
- **Columns:** Aptitudes (6 phases: Perceptiva, Reflexiva, Criativa, Retentiva, Ex. Simbólica, Ex. prática)
- **Rows:** Values (8 categories: Vitais, Estéticos, Técnicos, Intelectuais, Prudenciais, Sociais, Individuais, Religiosos)

### How to Use:
1. For each topic/theme to be taught, the teacher selects the most effective activities and didactic resources
2. Teacher fills cells indicating which aptitudes and which values should be especially developed
3. Teacher formulates evaluation criteria

### Quality Check:
**If any aptitude or value cell remains empty** → Planning incompletely achieves education objectives
- **Action Required:** Revise the list of activities OR account for those values to be filled by other topics in the subject/discipline

## Integration: Intellectual Formation + Human Formation

### Key Insight:
**Intelligence is not a capacity disconnected from the human person.**
- Intellectual formation is **condition and foundation** of integral formation
- Mental formation is the **base** to reach aesthetic, technical, ethical, and religious formation
- The three phases of the "reactive stage" offer clear foundation for non-strictly-intellectual educational process manifestations

### The Reactive Stage and Human Formation:

All manifestations of human life in the experimental field reduce to:
- **Expression** (Comunicativa phase)
- **Creation** (Extensiva phase)  
- **Use of things and oneself** (Prática phase)

**Critical Connection to Values:**
The **Prática (Practical) phase** includes specific functions of:
- **«Valorizar» (Valuing)**
- **«Eleger» (Choosing)**

These are **initial activities of human affective life**, since all human works arise as immediate consequence of valuing and selective functions.

**Conclusion:** These other manifestations of life (aesthetic to religious) **fit and can be ordered in the same objective system** that orders intellectual development.

## Inviolable Principles for Arete Implementation

### 1. Three Components Always Present
Every lesson plan MUST include:
- ✅ Conhecimentos (specific to subject)
- ✅ Aptidões (mapped to 6 phases)
- ✅ Valores/Virtudes (human formation)

### 2. Organic Cooperation
Activities should:
- Allow phases to feed each other (observation → reflection)
- Reinforce across phases (application → memory)

### 3. No Fragmentation
- System must provide **unity** to educational process
- Avoid treating subjects as isolated silos
- Connect intellectual formation with human formation

### 4. Teacher as Facilitator
- System should **simplify**, not complicate, teacher's work
- Give clarity to "educational gaze"
- Provide structure without rigidity

### 5. Values Through Well-Done Work
- Virtudes developed when teacher **demands excellence and care** (capricho e tarefa bem-feita)
- The three phases of reactive stage = fertile field for human formation
- Values emerge from **doing**, not just knowing
